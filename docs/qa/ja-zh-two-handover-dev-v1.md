# Japanese handover cases for #325

These three public development cases cover two actions in one sentence. Luna wrote the Japanese inputs and the Taiwan Traditional Chinese references for this repository under MIT. The [issue record](https://github.com/faberline/nlp/issues/325#issuecomment-7044131915) identifies the behavior to check.

| Case | Meaning to keep |
| --- | --- |
| T01 | The teacher handed over two objects. Each object went to its stated recipient. |
| T02 | The clerk handed over different objects to two different customers. Keep their names and the role of each object. |
| T03 | The teacher handed over a map. The student asked for directions. Keep each actor with their action. |

Independent data review and a merge to main adopt the cases. Check the new cases on that main before measuring a rule change.
